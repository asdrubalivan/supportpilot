# Shipping — Old Pages Books

## Shipping options <!-- id: shipping-options -->

- **Standard shipping**: 3 to 5 business days, $4.99.
- **Express shipping**: 1 to 2 business days, $12.99.

Delivery times start counting when the order ships, not when it is placed. Orders usually ship within 1 business day.

## Free shipping <!-- id: free-shipping -->

Standard shipping is free for orders of $50 or more. The $50 threshold is calculated after discounts are applied. Express shipping is never free.

## Where we ship <!-- id: where-we-ship -->

We ship only within the country. We do not offer international shipping.

## Tracking <!-- id: tracking -->

Every order includes a tracking number, sent by email when the order ships. The order status in your account shows whether the order is processing, shipped, or delivered, and the delivery date once delivered.
