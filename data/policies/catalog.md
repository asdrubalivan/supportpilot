# Catalog — Old Pages Books <!-- id: overview -->

Old Pages Books is an online bookstore specializing in classic literature.

## What we sell <!-- id: what-we-sell -->

We sell new and second-hand editions of classic books in four sections:

- **Classic novels**: works such as *Pride and Prejudice*, *Don Quixote*, and *Crime and Punishment*.
- **Adventure and science fiction**: including the complete works of Jules Verne, H. G. Wells, and Robert Louis Stevenson.
- **Philosophy**: from Plato and Marcus Aurelius to Nietzsche.
- **Self-help**: classic self-improvement titles such as *How to Win Friends and Influence People* and *The Art of War*.

## Antique editions <!-- id: antique-editions -->

Antique editions are books printed more than 70 years ago. Each listing describes the condition of the copy (Fine, Very Good, Good, or Fair). Photos show the actual copy you will receive, not a stock image.

## Signed copies <!-- id: signed-copies -->

Signed copies carry an author or translator signature and include a certificate of authenticity. Signed copies are sold as unique items and are marked "Signed" on the product page.

## Book formats <!-- id: book-formats -->

All books are physical. We do not sell e-books or audiobooks.
