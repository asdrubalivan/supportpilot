# Returns and Refunds — Old Pages Books

## Return window <!-- id: return-window -->

You may return any eligible book within 30 days of the delivery date. The delivery date is the date shown in your order tracking, not the purchase date. Returns requested after 30 days from delivery are not accepted.

## Non-returnable items <!-- id: non-returnable-items -->

Signed copies cannot be returned, because each one is a unique item. This applies even within the 30-day return window.

## Damaged books <!-- id: damaged-books -->

If a book arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo. Books that arrive damaged are accepted for return or replacement even if you report them after the standard return conditions would otherwise apply. Books damaged by the customer after delivery are not accepted for return.

## Refunds <!-- id: refunds -->

Refunds go to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear after we receive the returned book. Shipping costs are not refunded, except for books that arrived damaged.

## Returns from promotional orders <!-- id: returns-from-promotional-orders -->

If you return books from an order that received the "Buy 5, get the cheapest free" promotion, and the order is left with fewer than 5 books, the promotion no longer applies. In that case, the price of the book that was free is deducted from your refund.
