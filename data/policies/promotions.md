# Promotions — Old Pages Books

## Jules Verne discount <!-- id: jules-verne-discount -->

All books written by Jules Verne have a 20% discount, in any edition.

## Self-help discount <!-- id: self-help-discount -->

All books in the Self-help section have a 50% discount.

## Buy 5, get the cheapest free <!-- id: buy-5-get-the-cheapest-free -->

When an order contains 5 or more books, the cheapest book in the order is free. Only one book per order is free, regardless of how many books the order contains.

## Promotions cannot be combined <!-- id: promotions-cannot-be-combined -->

Promotions are not cumulative: only one promotion applies to each order. At checkout, the system automatically applies the single promotion that gives the customer the largest saving. For example, a Jules Verne book that is part of a 5-book order does not get both the 20% discount and the free-book promotion.

## Exclusions <!-- id: exclusions -->

Signed copies are excluded from all promotions.
