# Help desk (frontend/app/page.tsx)

Scope: the single-turn support page of SupportPilot, dressed as Old Pages Books. Mode: Operate.
Audience: a store customer asking one question; an evaluator checking why the agent answered what it did.
Task: pick the demo customer, ask, read the answer, inspect the cited passages and what was retrieved.
Constraints: single turn; answers take 10-20 s; customer selector visible (Ada Reader #1, Basil Bookworm #2); never imply a real business.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every policy document is a bound library volume; a citation is its spine, pulled from the shelf. Refuses the chat-bubble widget with grey source chips.

OWN-WORLD: Library buckram. Black-green buckram rail, linen-grey ground, cool near-white working panels. Each document owns one cloth: returns oxblood, shipping navy, promotions mustard, catalog plum. Gold-blocked numerals on the rail. Spine labels are small stamped rectangles carrying only the catalog number.

STORY: The visitor asks in plain words, gets a short answer whose every policy claim carries a coloured spine label, sees the shelf of what the agent pulled, and opens any spine to read the exact passage.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail across the top with store name, help desk, and the customer card switcher on the right. Left column: question box with Ask as the primary action and example questions. Right column: the answer, then the shelf: retrieved spines on one baseline, height by retrieval rank, cited spines pulled forward, the opened passage beneath.

FORM: Library Buckram, position 3 of my ordered list, seed key 82c799de. Raises: rank ramp (star atlas), shared baseline (botanical folio), colour as legend with catalog number as only label (factory catalog). Signature interaction: spines slide onto the shelf and cited ones pull forward; clicking a spine or inline label opens its passage.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
